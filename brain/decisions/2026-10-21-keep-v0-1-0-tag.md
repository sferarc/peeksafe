# Keep the accidental v0.1.0 tag

Recorded 2026-10-21.

## Decision

The annotated tag `v0.1.0` stays. It was pushed by accident through `push.followTags` and points at the release commit `ff97abc`. `push.followTags` is set to `false` in the checkout (`git config push.followTags false`), and `CONTRIBUTING.md` and [[development/release]] tell contributors not to enable it. Agents never tag.

## Why

The tag sits on the commit that was published, which is where the owner would have put it. Deleting and recreating it would rewrite a public ref for no gain, and `release.yml` fails safely at "already published" for 0.1.0 ([[2026-10-02-first-release-pending]]).

## What would reopen it

The tag pointing at a commit other than the published one, or the trusted publisher configuration needing a different tag.
